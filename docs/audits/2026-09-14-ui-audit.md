# NitiQuant UI audit — 14 September 2026

## Verified

Authenticated against production, navigated all eight views, and logged out. No production Run, Stop, Halt, Resume, Approve or Reject was submitted.

Home, Bank Nifty, Cash Portfolio, Archived Strategies, Operations, Research, Risk Controls and Decision Graph: navigation and visibility checked at 1440px and 390px; no page-width overflow or uncaught JavaScript exceptions.

Local isolated browser: route isolation, Back/Forward, reload, graph selection, missing-data state and mobile overflow passed.

`node --test tests/*.test.cjs`: 23 passing tests. Covers paper Run request payloads, duplicate control, Stop failure recovery, signal controls, chart timeframes, stale data, text escaping, reports, learning labels, sync failures, network timeout, safety serialization, research deduplication, hero status and login timeout.

## Fixed locally

- Failed Bank Nifty Stop left its button disabled: restore previously available Stop without retrying mutations automatically; keep Run disabled pending sync.
- Login could hang forever and accept repeated submits: 15-second abort plus duplicate guard and recoverable error.
- Halt and Resume could overlap: serialize the pair and disable both until completion.
- Research Enter submissions could overlap: ignore submissions while the request is pending.
- Decision Graph refreshes could overlap: serialize refresh and restore button after failure.
- Risk Controls implied global coverage: label legacy cash scope and direct users to Bank Nifty Stop & exit.
- CI omitted newer frontend tests: discover all `.test.cjs` files.

## Limits and remaining work

This is not a claim that the product is bug-free. Production trading mutations, broker market-time behavior, restart reconciliation, fill economics, full backend regressions and load/performance were not exercised in this audit. Existing tests simulate action requests; they are not evidence that every backend failure mode works. Exchange-calendar integration and the historical unresolved position remain separate operational work.

Fixes are not deployed as part of this audit.
